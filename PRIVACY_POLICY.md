# Privacy Policy for Simple QR Generator

**Last Updated: November 2, 2026**

## Overview

Simple QR Generator ("the App") is developed by Rahil Tiwari. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the App. The short version: **everything stays on your PC.** The App has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers.

## Information the App handles

- **Content you enter** (web addresses, Wi-Fi details, contact cards, payment IDs and so on) is turned into QR codes on your device. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Images you scan** (opened files, pasted images or screen captures) are decoded on your device and never uploaded.
- **Logos you add** are stored inside your saved styles and history on your device.
- **This PC's Wi-Fi details** (network name, security type and saved password) are read from Windows only when you click "Use this PC's Wi-Fi", only to fill in the Wi-Fi form. They are never sent anywhere. Like anything you type, they are kept in your history and last session on this PC if those settings are on. The App does not use your location; Windows may ask for location permission because it treats Wi-Fi network names as location data.

## Data stored on your device

The App keeps the following in its private local folder, which only you can access:

- History of codes you saved, copied or marked as favorite (you can turn this off or clear it in Settings)
- Styles you saved as presets
- The last code and design you were working on, so the App can reopen it (you can turn this off in Settings)
- Your settings, and whether you own Pro
- An error log used only to diagnose crashes; it never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it to us

Uninstalling the App, or using Settings → Apps → Simple QR Generator → Reset, deletes all of this.

## In-app purchases

Simple QR Generator Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Payment and account details are handled entirely by Microsoft; we never see your card, UPI or payment information. The App only asks the Microsoft Store whether your account owns Pro. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the purchase: https://privacy.microsoft.com/

## Third parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

## Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last Updated" date above will change too.
